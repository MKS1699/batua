# Batua — Expense & Budget Tracker

> **Your money, in one place.**

Batua (बटुआ) is a local-first personal finance app designed to help people manage their money simply and confidently.

Track income. Record expenses. Plan budgets. Stay in control.

## What is Batua?

Batua means _wallet_ in Hindi. A wallet is a familiar, neutral place where we keep our money together. Similarly, Batua aims to bring personal finances into one organized place—not just spending, but the complete picture of money coming in and going out.

Built with an India-first audience in mind and designed with global potential, Batua aims to make everyday money management straightforward, accessible, and privacy-conscious.

## Core Focus

- **Income** — Record and keep track of money coming in.
- **Expenses** — Track spending and understand where money goes.
- **Budgets** — Plan spending, manage limits, and make informed financial decisions.

## Product Vision

Batua is being developed with a local-first approach, prioritizing control over personal financial data.

The long-term vision includes:

- Multiple currencies for flexible money management.
- Organized financial summaries and visualizations.
- Data export and import for portability.
- Optional encrypted backups to user-owned cloud storage.
- Collaborative budgets that allow people to manage shared finances with appropriate permissions.

These are planned directions and should not be considered available features until implemented.
